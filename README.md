# VORQIX.AI Launchpad

Build a premium, conversion-focused marketing website for VORQIX.AI, an AI innovation company that develops intelligent software, automation systems, business tools, AI agents, custom software, and growth systems for entrepreneurs, startups, agencies, logistics companies, and enterprises. Create a modern, enterprise-grade site with a dark mode aesthetic, electric blue accents, glassmorphism panels, smooth motion, strong typography, and mobile-first responsiveness. Use a clean, high-trust design inspired by Apple, Stripe, OpenAI, Notion, and Vercel.

Include these sections: hero with headline, subheadline, primary and secondary CTA buttons, and a trust bar; services grid for AI SaaS development, business automation, AI agents, custom software, website/app development, and growth systems; featured products including SaaS Factory AI, Nexus Dispatch Pro, Compliance Scan AI, RecruitIQ AI, and ContentForge AI; benefits/statistics section; testimonials; how-it-works/process section; latest insights/blog preview; about section; strong final CTA; and footer with navigation plus social links. Add a contact form, lead capture, newsletter signup, analytics-ready setup, SEO metadata, accessibility support, and production-ready structure. Aim for a venture-backed SaaS brand feel with polished spacing, subtle gradients, and conversion-oriented copy throughout.
